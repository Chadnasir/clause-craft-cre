Clause Craft — start here

CRE lease and purchase agreement review. Runs on your computer; your contracts are not uploaded anywhere.

1. Use it in your browser Nothing to install

Works offline, straight from this folder.

  1. Double-click Clause Craft.html (it opens in Edge or Chrome).
  2. Click Upload and pick your lease or PSA (.docx), or drag the file onto the page.
  3. Pick your side and click Start review. Accept the redlines you want.
  4. Click Download .docx. It opens in Word with every change as a tracked change.

On another computer, open chadnasir.github.io/clause-craft-cre. It's the same tool, and it still runs on that computer.

2. Add it to Word on this PC Desktop Word

For Microsoft 365, or Word 2016 or later, on Windows.

  1. Double-click Install in Word. If Windows warns you about the file, click More info, then Run anyway.
  2. If Word is open, save your work and press Y. Word restarts and opens a demo lease with the Clause Craft panel on the right.
  3. After that, open any lease or PSA and click Home › Review Contract.

Word needs an internet connection to open the Clause Craft panel, which loads from GitHub Pages. The review itself runs inside Word.

3. Word on the web OneDrive

  1. Open your document in Word on the web.
  2. Click Home › Add-ins › More Settings › Upload My Add-in.
  3. Click Browse, pick Clause Craft manifest.xml from this folder, then click Upload.
  4. Click Home › Review Contract.

Word on the web keeps the add-in until you clear that browser's history and cache. If that happens, upload the manifest again.

What's in the panel

  • Review: risky clauses with suggested redlines, missing clauses, and checks on dates, rent math, defined terms and section references. You can review the whole document or just a selection.
  • Playbook: your own numbers (late charge, cure periods, deposit, due diligence and more), checked against the document and redlined to your standard. Click Edit standards to set them.
  • Draft: option to extend, early termination, free rent, TI allowance and more, written with your numbers. You can also save language you like to My clauses.
  • Ask: questions about the document, with the section cited. Explain selected text gives a plain-English read of any clause.
  • Summary: key terms, plus an issues list for the other side that you can insert or copy into an email.

If something doesn't work

  • No Review Contract button: make sure every Word window was closed, then reopen Word. Check that Word isn't still running in Task Manager (WINWORD.EXE).
  • The panel stays blank: check the internet connection, then close and reopen the panel.
  • Comments and tracked changes: these need Microsoft 365, Word 2024 or Word on the web. Older versions of Word show redlines as red and blue text instead.
  • Work computer: your IT settings may block add-ins. The browser version (step 1) still works.
  • Remove it: double-click Uninstall from Word.cmd.
Clause Craft is a rule-based first pass, not legal advice. Have counsel review before signing.
