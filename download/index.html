Get Clause Craft on your PC and in Word

CRE lease and purchase agreement review. Free, works offline, and your contracts stay on your computer.

Download for Windows ZIP file, under 100 KB

Add it to Word on your PC (about 1 minute)

  1. DownloadClick Download for Windows above. The file goes to your Downloads folder.
  2. UnzipOpen Downloads, right-click Clause-Craft-for-Windows, choose Extract All, then Extract. A Clause Craft folder opens.
  3. Plug it into WordIn that folder, double-click Install in Word. When it says Clause Craft is installed, press any key to close the window.

    If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. If it asks "Do you want to run this file?", click Run.

  4. Restart WordClose every Word window, then open Word again and open a lease or purchase agreement.
  5. Open Clause CraftOn the Home tab, click Review Contract. The Clause Craft panel opens on the right.

Word on the web (OneDrive)

1. Download the add-in file (Clause-Craft-manifest.xml).

2. In Word on the web, click Home › Add-ins › More Settings › Upload My Add-in.

3. Click Browse, pick that file from Downloads, then click Upload. Then click Home › Review Contract.

No Word handy?

Double-click Clause Craft.html in the folder, or open the browser version. Upload your .docx, review it, then click Download .docx. Your redlines come back as Word tracked changes.

If something doesn't work

  • No Review Contract button: make sure every Word window was closed, then reopen Word.
  • Blank panel: Word needs an internet connection to open the panel. The review itself runs inside Word.
  • Work computer: your IT settings may block add-ins. The browser version still works.
  • Remove it: double-click Uninstall from Word in the same folder.
